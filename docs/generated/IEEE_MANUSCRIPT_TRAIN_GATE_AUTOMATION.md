## Abstract

Unprotected and manually operated railway level crossings in developing rail corridors such as Bangladesh suffer fatal collision risks driven by delayed manual gate closure, visually degraded weather, un-synchronized multi-camera hardware, and loud mixed road-traffic acoustic noise. While deep vision and acoustic classifiers achieve high frame-level benchmarks on curated splits, they lack system-level interlocking semantics and fail dangerously under field domain shift or sparse frame dropouts. This paper presents and evaluates the **V3 Four-Layer Causal Multimodal Safety State Machine (`CrossingStateMachine`, 11 states)** alongside a forensic audit of dataset split leakage, post-hoc acoustic calibration/hysteresis and nested leave-one-event-out (LOEO) validation, optical-degraded acoustic wakeup, synthetic monsoon attenuation stress testing, and ARM CPU edge compute feasibility. First, a forensic audit of the project's perception datasets exposes hidden leakage inflating prior validation scores: `87.1%` (`411/472`) of test images in the active YOLOv8n split (`705d388743ea`, nominal `0.9946 mAP@50`) originated from offline train-augmented exports and **`27/29` (`93.1%`) 10-minute capture sessions** crossed splits (`447/447` timestamped test images shared sessions with training data), while **`5/57` YouTube locomotive horn stems (`28/86 = 32.56%` of positive recordings)** crossed splits in Audio V3 (`audio/v3`). We engineer non-destructive, strictly grouped candidate splits (`vision_v1_session_grouped`, `audio_v3_stem_grouped`) reducing cross-session (`<=600 s`) and cross-stem overlap to **`0.0%`**. Second, on `27` exploratory roadside crossing events (`pre-db40305` frozen inference caches against AI-assisted adopted references; official Stage-2.1 review pending), V3 eliminates false closures (`0/3` vs `3/3` stateless union, exact McNemar `p = 0.25`) and eliminates premature barrier reopening (`0/11` on synced clips vs `4/11` under V2 disappearance logic, exact McNemar `p = 0.125`) via an asymmetric **departure-plus-occupancy AND-gate** with zero backfill (`allow_stale_detection_backfill=False`). Third, acoustic sweep `Exp O` (`2,600` total field windows across all `27` events: `2,470` `audio23`, `116` `no_train`, `14` `engine`) and 23-fold nested LOEO cross-validation (`Exp R`) reveal that road-vehicle horns sustain `3–9` consecutive windows `>=0.37` (`max 0.826`) and collapse to single-window bursts at `>=0.75`, achieving out-of-fold specificity `0.6000` (`0.0000 -> 0.6000`) and point-estimate `5-bin ECE 0.2955 -> 0.0999` (`OOF Brier 0.2724 -> 0.2366`, paired Δ CI `[-0.0839, +0.0104]` crossing `0`) while lowering `F1` (`0.6857 -> 0.6400 [0.4167, 0.8148]`) and `Recall` (`0.9231 -> 0.6154`) and remaining strictly non-gating by default. Fourth, `100` synthetic weather-attenuation × burst-dropout runs (`Exp Q`) plus `18` fault injections (`Exp L`) achieve **`0` unsafe openings** and **`100%` legal transitions**, while an opt-in synthetic optical-degraded acoustic wakeup (`RULE_L3_OPTICAL_DEGRADED_AUDIO_WAKEUP`, `Exp R`, `SYNTHETIC_ONLY`) closes the `alpha = 0.35` (`conf = 0.308 < 0.35`) empty-frame optical cliff (`0% -> 100%` at `4.0 s`) with `0/2` false closures on ambient/impulse controls. Finally, measured Apple M1 CPU profiling (`Exp P` and standalone TorchScript thread scaling) quantifies **`-74.91%`** checkpoint size stripping (`24.49 -> 6.15 MB`), **`2.06×`** end-to-end `YOLO.predict` speedup at `416px` (`44.56 -> 21.60 ms`, `46.30 FPS`; `16.84 ms` @ 4T TorchScript + `16.98 ms` OpenCV `1080p->rotate90->416px` preprocess), **`0.0381 ms/tick`** V3 FSM overhead, and **`96.56%–98.64%`** daily vision frame reduction under event-triggered wakeup.

**Index Terms:** Railway level-crossing safety, multimodal sensor fusion, finite-state interlocking, dataset leakage audit, fail-closed edge AI, mixed road-traffic acoustics.

## I. Introduction

Railway level crossings remain acute public safety bottlenecks in densely populated corridors across Bangladesh, where irregular train speeds, roadside encroachment, manual gatekeeper reaction latency, and high road-traffic horn density combine to elevate crash severity [Ibtihal, 2026; Bangladesh Railway Safety Study, 2021]. Recent computer-vision and acoustic studies apply convolutional or YOLO-family detectors directly to trackside cameras [Fayyaz & Johnson, 2020; Ning et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2022] and log-Mel spectrograms [Tran et al., 2022] to recognize trains or horns. However, safety-critical railway interlocking research grounded in Petri-net formalisms [Durmuş et al., 2010] emphasizes that **safety arises from asymmetric state retention rather than raw frame-level accuracy**: a single missed frame or flickering disappearance must never reopen a lowered barrier while a train occupies the island, nor may roadside truck or bus horns falsely lock gates closed all day.

Inspections of early crossing AI prototypes reveal three recurring methodological blind spots:
1. **Validation Leakage Masking Field Shift:** Scraping consecutive screenshot bursts or exporting augmented YOLO crops before splitting leaks near-identical views across training and evaluation sets, inflating nominal metrics (e.g., `0.9946 mAP@50`) while hiding brittle generalization under `-90°` container rotations, illumination shifts, and road-traffic clutter.
2. **Disappearance-Based Premature Reopening:** When vision detectors flicker during long freight passages or heavy monsoon obscuration, stateless rules or disappearance timers reopen gates prematurely while wagons remain inside the crossing box.
3. **Unconstrained Continuous Edge Execution:** Running `640×640` dual-camera neural inference continuously at `5–10 Hz` on low-cost ARM single-board computers (SBCs) saturates quad-core CPUs thermally and electrically unless combined with lightweight resolution scaling, weight stripping, and event-triggered sensor wakeup.

To close these gaps cleanly while enforcing strict data-governance discipline (zero unapproved raw-media decoding under [`media_guard.py`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/src/railway_detection/safety/media_guard.py)), this work makes four concrete, reproducible contributions:
1. **Formal V3 Four-Layer Causal State Machine ([`decision_engine_v3_four_layer.py`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/src/railway_detection/fusion/decision_engine_v3_four_layer.py)):** Decouples independent approach confirmation (Layer 1 front), lateral crossing motion persistence (`SideMovementTracker`, Layer 2), strictly non-gating acoustic reinforcement (Layer 3), conservative rear/tail exit confirmation (Layer 4), and explicit 5-state island occupancy verification (`OccupancyVerifier`), backed by causal snapshot alignment without stale backfill (`Exp A–N`).
2. **Algorithmic Dataset Leakage Forensics & Clean Split Synthesis (`data/splits/`):** Quantifies exact session-burst (`93.1%`) and augmentation (`87.1%`) leakage in Vision V1 (`705d388743ea`) and YouTube stem leakage (`32.56%`) in Audio V3, generating zero-leakage `vision_v1_session_grouped` and `audio_v3_stem_grouped` splits.
3. **Field Acoustic Run-Length & Calibration Diagnostics (`Exp O`):** Demonstrates mechanistically across all `2,600` Layer-B field windows (`2,470` `audio23` + `116` `no_train` + `14` `engine`) why consecutive hysteresis (`k=2`) fails at `tau=0.37` against `3–9`-window road horns, quantifies exact recall-specificity Pareto frontiers up to `0/3` traffic-horn false alarms at `(0.75, k=2)`, and evaluates temperature calibration (`ECE 0.2955 -> 0.0709`).
4. **Synthetic Monsoon Stress Cliffs (`Exp Q`) & Measured CPU Edge Profiling (`Exp P`):** Exposes a silent healthy-empty-frame optical miss vulnerability at `alpha = 0.35`, alongside empirical Apple M1 CPU latencies and transparent analytical duty-cycle projections (`-96.6%` to `-98.6%` daily frames) toward Raspberry Pi deployment.

## II. Related Work and Research Gaps

- **Railroad Acoustic Warning Detection:** Tran et al. (2022) [`10.3390/s22124453`](https://doi.org/10.3390/s22124453) demonstrated high-accuracy train horn classification via log-Mel CNNs on clean isolated segments. Our field evaluation (`Exp C`, `Exp O`) demonstrates that un-adapted ESC-50-negative CNNs fire on `100%` (`10/10`) of horn-absent crossing clips and `3/3` train-free Bangladesh traffic clips (`0.800–0.826` max score), proving acoustics cannot serve as a standalone barrier trigger.
- **Vision-Only Level-Crossing & Intrusion Detection:** Fayyaz & Johnson (2020) [`10.3390/mi11121055`](https://doi.org/10.3390/mi11121055) and Ning et al. (2024) [`10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3504010`](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3504010) advance rail/crossing object localization and segmentation risk scoring, while IA-YOLO [Liu et al., 2022, `10.1609/aaai.v36i2.20072`] adapts filters to fog/low light and SynRailObs [Guo & Rambach, 2025, `arXiv:2505.10784`] provides synthetic rare obstacles. None formalize causal 11-state gate-boom interlocking under noisy multi-camera dropouts, orientation faults, and contradictory occupancy signals.
- **Fail-Safe Crossing Interlocking:** Durmuş et al. (2010) [`10.3182/20100830-3-CZ-2001.00059`](https://doi.org/10.3182/20100830-3-CZ-2001.00059) modeled fail-safe crossing states with Petri nets assuming reliable discrete track circuits. Our V3 architecture bridges noisy deep perception to deterministic fail-closed interlocking transitions (`ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS`) despite un-genlocked feeds and missing sensor streams.

## III. Four-Layer System Architecture & Safety State Machine (`V3`)

Fig. 1 and Fig. 2 ([`fig01`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/docs/generated/final_research_v2/figures/fig01_system_architecture.png), [`fig02`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/docs/generated/final_research_v2/figures/fig02_four_layer_workflow.png)) illustrate the modular architecture across `src/railway_detection/`:

1. **Media Orientation & Feature Parity (`vision/video.py`, `audio/features.py`):** Orthogonal container display-rotation normalization (`0°, ±90°, ±180°, ±270°, ±360°`) guarantees upright RGB frames (`7/7` angles verified with CPU `YOLOv8n`), while `compute_log_mel_spectrogram(..., log_scale="natural")` matches V3 training statistics (`[64, 61]` shape at `16 kHz`, `n_fft=512`, `hop=256`).
2. **Causal Snapshot Collection & Health (`fusion/collector.py`, `fusion/source_tracker.py`):** Aligns asynchronous detections within `temporal_tolerance_s = 0.50 s` at `dt = 0.20 s` (`5 Hz`). Setting `allow_stale_detection_backfill = False` ensures each observation is consumed at most once, preventing phantom multi-tick persistence across dropped frames (`Exp I` reduced spurious B4 triggers `16 -> 10`). `SourceTracker` classifies every stream into `HEALTHY`, `DEGRADED`, `STALE` (`> 2.0 s`), `MISSING`, or `RECOVERING`.
3. **Asymmetric Four-Layer Role Separation (`fusion/rules_v3_four_layer.py`, `occupancy.py`):**
   - **Layer 1 (Approach — Front Camera):** Enters `WARNING` only after `confirm_frames = 2` consecutive frames with viewpoint confidence `>= 0.35`, filtering single-frame glints (`Exp J`: `0/3` `no_train` FPs vs `3/3` stateless).
   - **Layer 2 (Transit — Side Movement Tracker):** Classifies crossing-box presence into `{MOVING, STOPPED, SINGLE_FRAME, UNOBSERVED}`, latching stalled trains/obstructions inside `CROSSING_OCCUPIED`.
   - **Layer 3 (Support-Only — Audio CNN):** Forbidden from triggering barrier closure or authorizing opening; strictly reinforces active presence and extends `CROSSING_CLEAR` quiet dwell (`audio_hold_s = 1.5 s`).
   - **Layer 4 + Occupancy AND-Gate (`REAR_CONFIRMED` & `OccupancyState.CLEAR`):** Under **`V3_CONSERVATIVE_POSITIVE`**, reopening strictly requires positive rear/tail observation **AND** uncontradicted `OccupancyState.CLEAR` **AND** quiet Layer 3 dwell **AND** healthy clearance sensors. Any sensor outage, timeout, EOF during passage, or conflict (`OccupancyState.CONTRADICTORY`) latches `FAULT_FAIL_SAFE` (`CLOSED`).
4. **11-State FSM & Gate Boom Model (`state_machine.py`, `gate_model.py`, `runner.py`):** Enforces explicit legal edges across `{IDLE, TRAIN_APPROACHING, WARNING (2.0 s), GATE_CLOSING (3.0 s, 90°->0°, blocked <=45°), TRAIN_PRESENT, CROSSING_OCCUPIED, TRAIN_DEPARTING, REAR_CONFIRMED, CROSSING_CLEAR (1.5 s), GATE_OPENING (2.0 s), FAULT_FAIL_SAFE}`. *(Simulation Note: `EventSimulationRunner` steps `PhysicalGateModel` at the issuing tick `t` [Convention A], shifting logged actuation milestones `-0.20 s` relative to post-tick Convention B stepping.)*

## IV. Forensic Dataset Audit, Leakage Discovery & Remediation

Strict inspection across manifests (zero raw media opened) resolved long-standing metric paradoxes ([Table 1](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/results/publication_tables/PUBLICATION_TABLES.md), [`fig11`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/docs/generated/figures/fig11_dataset_leakage_comparison.png)):

1. **Vision V1 Split Leakage (`baler.v1i.yolov8`, `3,157` images / `1,315` unique source groups / `0` negatives):** Contrary to external estimates of `~4,000` training frames (`"104 rainy"` verified `NOT FOUND`), the corpus contains `3,267` boxes (`front: 1,761`, `side: 805`, `rear: 701`). Parsing filename timestamps (`3,077` timestamped images across `13` dates `2025-11-13`..`2026-07-11`) reveals **`29` discrete capture sessions (`gap <= 600 s`)**. Both original Roboflow folders and the active training split [`705d388743ea`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/data/splits/705d388743ea/split_summary.json) leak **`27/29` (`93.1%`)** sessions across splits (`447/447` [`100%`] of timestamped `705d/test` images share sessions with `train`), and `705d/test` further drew **`411/472` (`87.1%`)** of its samples from Roboflow `train/` 3×-augmented exports. Thus frozen `YOLOv8n`'s `0.9946 mAP@50` measured same-burst memorization, explaining its drop to `43.48%` (`10/23`) real approach triggering on unrotated crossing videos (`13` misses: `7` `-90°` zero-detection `.mov`s, `5` sparse-gap suppressions, `1` late rear mismatch).
2. **Audio V3 Positive Stem Leakage (`audio/v3`, `172` clips):** While ESC-50 negatives strictly maintain 5-fold isolation (`0` fold overlap), **`5/57` unique YouTube horn stems (`28/86 = 32.56%` of positive recordings)** straddle `train/val/test`.
3. **Layer B Field Confounding & Missing Modalities (`27` active events):** Every single `23/23` `train_events` proposal in [`RESEARCHER_ANNOTATION_ADOPTION.json`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/docs/generated/professor_evidence/mission43/RESEARCHER_ANNOTATION_ADOPTION.json) shares `{crossing_A, session_20260914_morning, day, overcast}`, whereas all `3/3` `no_train` and `1/1` `engine` clips share `{night, clear}` — `100%` confounding class with environment. Moreover, `0/27` have side cameras, `0/27` have occupancy sensors (`1/27` has proposal `T_scene_clear = 32.5 s`), and `0/27` yield `last_carriage` boxes.
4. **Constructive Remediation (`leakage_audit.py`):** Without overwriting frozen checkpoint manifests, we synthesized [`vision_v1_session_grouped`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/data/splits/vision_v1_session_grouped/split_summary.json) (`train=1,776 [738 unique]`, `val=336`, `test=241`, `804` eval aug duplicates removed, **`0/29` crossing sessions up to `gap=600 s`**, `0` multi-split groups) and [`audio_v3_stem_grouped`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/data/splits/audio_v3_stem_grouped/split_summary.json) (`60/60`, `13/13`, `13/13` balanced with **`0/57` cross-split stems**). Retraining awaits `AUTH-MEDIA-ACCESS.json` approval (`ready: false`, `7` official blockers).

### B. Post-Hoc Acoustic Hysteresis & Calibration (`Exp O`, [`fig12`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/docs/generated/figures/fig12_audio_hysteresis_and_calibration.png))
Across all `2,600` Layer-B windows (`2,470` in `audio23` + `116` in `no_train` + `14` in `engine`):
- Frozen `(tau=0.37, k=1)` yields `Rec=0.9231` (`12/13`), `Spec=0.0000` (`10/10` FP), `Prec=0.5455`, `F1=0.6857`, and `3/3` `no_train` FPs (`max 0.800–0.826`).
- Run-length tracing proves why hysteresis alone at `0.37` fails: `no_train` traffic horns sustain **`3, 5, and 9` consecutive windows `>=0.37`**, yet collapse to **`longest_run_ge_0_75 = 1` isolated window `>=0.75`**.
- Raising the gate to **`(tau=0.75, k=2)`** suppresses **`3/3` `no_train` FPs (`0` engine FP)** and elevates `audio23` **`Spec=0.9000` (`9/10`), `Prec=0.8889`, `Acc=0.7391`, `F1=0.7273`**, at an unavoidable recall cost (`0.9231 -> 0.6154`). Exploratory temperature scaling (`T=3.0`) drops 5-bin event-max `ECE 0.2955 -> 0.0709` and `Brier 0.2724 -> 0.2246`.

### C. Optical Attenuation × Burst-Dropout Stress (`Exp Q`, [`fig14`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/docs/generated/figures/fig14_weather_attenuation_stress_heatmap.png))

- **`0/100` unsafe barrier openings** and **`100%` legal FSM transitions**.
- Closure holds at **`100%` (`80/80`)** for `alpha >= 0.45` (`eff_conf >= 0.396`) even under `50%` random frame drops.
- Crucially, at **`alpha = 0.35` (`eff_conf = 0.308 < 0.35`)**, `closure_rate` drops sharply from `100% -> 0%` (`miss_open_rate = 1.0`, `failsafe = 0.0`): because `camera_1` continues streaming valid *empty* frames without dropping packets, heartbeat watchdogs (`SourceTracker`) stay `HEALTHY`, leaving V3 in `IDLE/OPEN`. This identifies an architectural blind spot requiring image-blur/visibility monitoring or diverse non-optical approach sensing (vibration/radar).
- `stalled_obstacle_during_departure` safely locks `CLOSED` until `22.6 s` after clearing at `20.8 s`; `multi_train_reopen_interrupt` safely reverses `GATE_OPENING -> GATE_CLOSING` upon second train arrival at `14.8 s`.

### D. Nested LOEO Acoustic Cross-Validation & Optical-Degraded Wakeup (`Exp R`, [`fig15`](file:///Users/sayemuddin/Desktop/Train_automation/docs/generated/figures/fig15_exp_r_loeo_and_optical_wakeup.png))

- **23-Fold Nested LOEO Audio CV (`n = 23` `audio23` events, `2,000`-resample stratified bootstrap):** Fitting temperature scaling and `(tau, k)` strictly on the `22` training folds before scoring each held-out event yields out-of-fold **`Specificity = 0.6000` (`6/10`, bootstrap `[0.3000, 0.9000]` / Wilson `[0.3127, 0.8318]` vs `0.0000` frozen baseline)**, **`Precision = 0.6667`**, and **`Accuracy = 0.6087`**, while trading away sensitivity and F1 relative to the uncalibrated `tau=0.37` baseline (**`F1 = 0.6400 [0.4167, 0.8148]` vs `0.6857`**, **`Recall = 0.6154` vs `0.9231`**). Point-estimate calibration error improves (**OOF `Brier 0.2724 -> 0.2366`**, **`5-bin ECE 0.2955 -> 0.0999`**), though the paired `cal_minus_raw_brier` 95% CI **`[-0.0839, +0.0104]` crosses `0`**, `9/23` original folds saturated at `T = 4.0` (uncapping to `T <= 15` gives `max T = 4.2`, `Brier = 0.2379`, `ECE = 0.1007`), and calibrated predictions leave `[0.0, 0.4)` empty. Removing `-nt_fp` from fold selection alters `(tau, k)` on `event_003` and `event_023` while preserving identical OOF metrics (`(tau=0.75, k=2)` selected in `20/23` original folds, `18/23` strict train-only).
- **Opt-In Optical-Degraded Audio Wakeup (`RuleIdV3.RULE_L3_OPTICAL_DEGRADED_AUDIO_WAKEUP`):** Guarded behind `enable_optical_degradation_audio_wakeup = False` (leaving default V3 behavior byte-identical), activating ONLY when optical visibility drops below `0.45` (`optical_degraded`) **AND** `degraded_audio_wakeup_threshold >= 0.75` persists across `>= 3` consecutive frames **AND** crossing occupancy is not confirmed `CLEAR`. On the `alpha = 0.35` (`conf = 0.308`) monsoon cliff, closure improves from **`0% -> 100%` (trigger @ `4.0 s`) with `0` unsafe openings**, while preserving **`0/2` false closures** under clear-sky sustained traffic horns and degraded-optics single-impulse horns.

- **End-to-End Stage Latency (`Exp P`, `4` threads):** E2E `YOLO.predict` speeds up **`2.06×`** from **`44.557 ms` (`22.44 FPS`) at `640px` to `21.597 ms` (`46.30 FPS`) at `416px`** (TorchScript forward exact match `0.0` drift: `36.92 / 16.91 / 11.49 ms` for `640/416/320`). Event-triggered camera wakeup (`60 s` active per train + `30 s` watchdog + continuous `2 Hz` audio) cuts daily vision frames **`-96.56%` to `-98.64%`** (`11,760–29,760 frames/day` for `10–40` trains/day), compressing projected `416px` RPi 4B utilization to **`0.0258–0.0506` cores** (`0.0113–0.0221` on RPi 5).

## VII. Limitations, Threats to Validity & Future Work

1. **Governance & Label Provenance:** Official Phase 12 promotion remains blocked (`ready: false`, `7` blockers, `0` Stage-2.1 human reviews, `AUTH-MEDIA-ACCESS.json` absent). Real-video replay reflects frozen `pre-db40305` unrotated caches against adopted AI-assisted event references (`Exp N`).
2. **Site, Weather & Hardware Coverage:** All `23` Layer B train clips come from one `day/overcast` morning crossing session; physical side cameras (`0/27`), island occupancy sensors (`0/27`), tail annotations (`0` `last_carriage`), and physical Raspberry Pi thermal/power benchmarks remain unmeasured.
3. **Prioritized Next Path:** Upon human media/Stage-2.1 authorization: (i) re-extract upright `.mov` streams via `video.py`; (ii) retrain Vision V2 (`imgsz=416` + tail labels + hard negatives) and Audio V4 (roadside traffic-horn negatives) on `vision_v1_session_grouped` and `audio_v3_stem_grouped`; (iii) integrate upstream non-optical wakeup (vibration/radar) + physical axle-counter occupancy on a multi-weather field crossing.

## VIII. Conclusion

True railway level-crossing automation in mixed-traffic corridors cannot rely on high nominal benchmark mAP or standalone acoustic triggers. Our audit demonstrates how `93.1%` session leakage and `32.56%` stem leakage concealed fragile generalization, while causal **V3 Four-Layer FSM** separation (`0/11` premature openings, `0/3` false closures, `0/100` weather-stress unsafe openings, `0.0381 ms/tick` logic cost) paired with leakage-free splits, nested LOEO-validated `(0.75, k=2)` acoustic filtering, opt-in optical-degraded acoustic wakeup, and `-96.56% to -98.64%` event-triggered edge duty cycling establishes a scientifically transparent, fail-closed foundation ready for supervised Stage-2.1 validation and hardware deployment trials.
