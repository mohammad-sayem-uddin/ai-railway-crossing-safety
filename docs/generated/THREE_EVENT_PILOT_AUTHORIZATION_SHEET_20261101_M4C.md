## Scope

This sheet authorizes only, if separately signed, a workflow pilot on the three named events. It does not authorize full 27-event review, ground-truth approval, promotion, inference, training, cache generation, reruns, or official evaluation.

## Required prerequisites

- Protocol authority/version decision recorded and released.
- Review-policy decisions D3(a)/D3(c) recorded or explicitly scoped for pilot use.
- Named human reviewers selected by the researcher; no identities are supplied here.
- Reviewer independence and model-firewall controls verified.
- Media-access authorization for the exact front/back sources.
- Queue/inventory read-only audit confirms the three cohort-qualified keys.
- Review tool/workflow release and hash approved.

## Permitted operations after signature

- Open and inspect only the three explicitly authorized events' front/back media.
- Observe required media hashes through the approved workflow.
- Measure actual review session start/end times.
- Record independent observations, six boundary states, frame evidence, rationales, uncertainty/censoring, and operational stop conditions.
- Produce temporary validation drafts or real review records only if the separate record-writing permission below is also signed.
- Escalate disagreements and perform an independently authorized second review.

## Prohibited operations

- Any event outside the three named keys.
- Model inference/training or consulting model outputs.
- Production cache generation, experiment execution, or reruns.
- Ground-truth modification or promotion.
- Promotion receipts.
- Official evaluation or EVAL-AUTH.
- Adding reviewers to the authoritative allowlist through this sheet.
- Applying unresolved R04 offsets or inventing timestamps.

## Completion criteria

For each event: identity and sources confirmed; reviewer independence attested; hashes observed; actual duration recorded; all six boundaries assigned permitted states with rationales; camera-local timing preserved; disagreements/stop conditions recorded; history validated; no approval or promotion implied. Pilot timing observations are evidence for researcher discussion only.

## Stop conditions

Stop immediately for wrong/missing source, hash mismatch/change, model-output exposure, protocol ambiguity, missing landmark, tooling defect, unauthorized identity, insufficient evidence, or any request to fabricate a value. Escalate to the researcher; do not repair silently.
