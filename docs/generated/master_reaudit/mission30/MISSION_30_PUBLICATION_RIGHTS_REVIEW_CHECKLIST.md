## Human release review: all items open

- [ ] Identify the actual person or authority entitled to approve release of the proposed original manuscript and supporting artifacts.
- [ ] Fix the exact text/version, output artifacts, recipients, purpose and proposed release channel; distinguish this task's Git delivery from a journal submission.
- [ ] Verify applicable rights and conditions for original prose, conceptual software descriptions and software-validation summaries. Do not infer an open-source license from repository presence.
- [ ] Review each historical numeric/provenance note for accurate attribution, required limitations and permitted output scope. Do not publish it as new empirical evidence.
- [ ] Review aggregate inventory descriptions without claiming acquisition signoff or verified sample sizes.
- [ ] Confirm that raw recordings, frames/audio extracts, production checkpoints/caches and private human records remain excluded.
- [ ] Confirm that no third-party full text, source-code bundle, copied figure or protected illustration has been reproduced; check short original paraphrases and citations separately.
- [ ] Review Tables 1–7, supplementary Table S1 and Figures 1–3 against their exact evidence mappings and actual release scope.
- [ ] Resolve actual relevant institutional/site/privacy/participant applicability for the proposed work/outputs; do not manufacture blanket approval or exemption.
- [ ] Approve truthful human authorship, contributions, conflicts, funding, AI-use, ethics and availability statements.
- [ ] Record the artifact-specific R-PUB determination, conditions and actual approving authority. No determination is supplied here.
- [ ] Check validity, expiry/revocation, recipients, redistribution and any output restrictions applicable to that real release decision.
- [ ] Obtain separate actual external-submission approval before submission; retain the existing raw-release denial and all operation-specific constraints.

Unresolved exact-source rights are not transformed into a manuscript release grant. Equally, the checklist does not invent a universal requirement to clear every empirical asset before humans may edit original source-free prose.
