# Removed universal barriers and stage corrections

**Eight bounded overbroad requirements removed/corrected, not eight fully closed inherited families.** Two are unsupported institution/site certificate gates. Five remove extrapolation of production-provider requirements to every offline research method. One corrects adjudicator timing for a separately approved first-pass-only stage. These actions do not waive existing canonical requirements, certify legal exemptions or adopt an alternative method.

Mission27's written activation sequence does explicitly appoint three roles and bind trusted adapters before the **full existing pilot**; the handbook stops formal use on unavailable trusted adapters. Those rules are not deleted. Its actual adjudication step needs sealed parents at the later dispute stage. Mission35 permits proportionate narrower work and researcher-controlled method proposals, but also forbids silent protocol changes. Therefore the narrower first-pass workflow is an explicit pending decision D35-OFFLINE, not a claim it already satisfies the original activation gate. Full canonical pilot readiness remains unestablished. Real identity/assignment, accurate timing, blinding, preserved originals, current source-use permission and traceability still matter under either actual method.

Unknown institutional/site/privacy context is a question, not a confirmed prohibition. Professor awareness is not a university policy, funding/participant relationship, consent, exemption or approval. No applicable institutional/site requirement has been demonstrated for the current private offline operation. Check actual known circumstances/constraints and escalate a precise affected use if a real rule/identifiable-material/participant condition is established. This does not assert that no people appear or that no rule can apply; reviewer participation remains an actual human input. Do not make a generic external approval package a default dependency for unrelated private source verification.

Ownership and scientific decision authority are already researcher-declared. Do not demand another recording-contributor transfer license, outsider permission to choose one's own research questions, independent legal certification of every personally recorded original, serial/GPS/absolute recording timezone, whole-cohort signoff or future promotion receipt as universal prerequisites to a narrowly scoped ten-member identity/PTS check. Retain exact member binding and the actual camera-local clock information required for the selected analysis. A scoped current permission is still needed and is prepared as AUTH35-V; no signature or grant is invented.

Public release/commercial/publication permissions, publisher adapter, optional auditor/hardware/R04/T2 and later dispute parents are not additional initial gates.
